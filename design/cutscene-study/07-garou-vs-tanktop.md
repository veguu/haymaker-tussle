## C. Character animation and posing

- Coil (20.69-21.15): Garou is in a deep crouch, torso square to camera, shoulders hunched forward, head up with eyes
  on camera. Both hands circle low in front of the hips (right hand from chest height at 20.69 down to the lower centre
  by 20.85-21.05), palms cupped. A held, compressed pose with only the hands moving. This is the whole wind-up.
- Thrust (21.655): side-on, the fist fully extended toward screen left, the other shoulder back. No in-between
  drawings of the arm travelling are shown: crouch, then extension.
- Target before contact (21.76-21.82): upright, chin down, eyes cut sideways to the incoming fist, jaw clenched.
  A reaction of seeing it coming, held 3 f.
- Contact (21.99-22.22): Tank-Top's face is turned 3/4 toward the fist. The fist sits on the front-side of his
  jaw/cheek on the side nearest Garou, and his head snaps back and up toward screen left. His shoulder stays square;
  the head leads and the body follows.
- Carry (22.26-22.72): Tank-Top is folded forward over Garou: chest square to Garou, arms flung forward past him
  (hands open), head pinned back by the fist. Garou drives low, his head at Tank-Top's chest, arm bent, legs behind
  (white trousers at the bottom right). It is one combined mass moving right to left.
- Wall break (22.76-22.92): Garou's arm straightens fully and his torso rises square to camera, the other arm flung
  back to the right for balance. Tank-Top sinks into the wall, hands up near his face. This is the follow-through: the
  arm reaches full extension only AT the wall, not at first contact.
- Flurry (18.32-19.92): Garou fights from close in, his back or side to camera, twisting from the hips. The other arm
  flings back with every blow, so there is overlap and counter-rotation. Tank-Top stays in place, chest square, arms
  wide, mouth open; he is not knocked away between blows, the blows just land on a body that stays put.
- Slip (6.74-7.44): Garou ducks forward and to his left under Tank-Top's overhand, his left hand on Tank-Top's
  forearm, then rotates his torso to his right so his head clears past the fist (7.31-7.44). This is a dodge built on a
  continuous turn, drawn on 3s.
- Grounded versus airborne: everything in the climax is grounded or carried. Nobody is launched into the air.
  Tank-Top's travel is a carry by the attacker's fist (Garou travels with him), so judging from the frames it is "root
  motion of both bodies together", not a free physics flight.

## E. What it teaches us

Strongest techniques
1. Shot ladder into the hit: coil, eyes, 1 f thrust, fist close-up, target's face, splash, impact frame, contact,
   carry, wall. Each shot shows one idea for 1-15 f.
2. The target looks at the fist before it lands (21.76). This pre-sets his facing so the contact reads as square.
3. The carry: the punch does not end at contact. The attacker drives the target across a locked, side-on frame into a
   wall, and the arm only straightens at the wall.
4. A speed ramp inside the carry: 5 f fast smear, 10 f slow hang, then the hit on the wall.
5. Slow parts move: every slow-motion or held shot has a new drawing every 2-3 f, plus drifting particles.

Principles to adopt
- Hide the arm's extension in 1-3 frames across cuts; show the LOADED pose (held) and the EXTENDED pose (impact),
  never a slow rotation between them.
- Put the long travel after contact (target and attacker together), or film travel side-on with the destination
  already in frame.
- Pre-turn the target toward the fist one shot before contact.
- The impact frame is 1 f; whatever comes after must already show the body displaced.

What would NOT translate to Roblox R6
- Water-hand abstractions, kanji card, line-art inversion frames, painted speed-line backgrounds: these are drawn 2D
  effects with no character on screen. They would need flat UI overlays and would read as a different art style.
- Faces: the climax leans on eyes (eye extreme close-up, glowing eyes, the target's sideways glance). R6 blocky heads
  have no eye direction; we can only aim the head or the camera.
- Squash/folding of the target over the fist (22.52): R6 torsos are rigid boxes, so the fold has to come from rotating
  the torso and limbs, not bending the spine.
- Blood geysers (and the "no gore" direction for this game) do not carry over.
- Smears of whole bodies: in Roblox, motion blur has to be faked (afterimage clones or a short trail), and done at 30-60
  fps it looks different from a drawn smear.

Concrete uses in our sequence
- Haymaker close-up: cut to the target's head turning toward the incoming fist (about 3 f, or 0.1 s) before the
  impact frames. Snap his root to face the attacker during that cut so the chest is square at contact.
- Replace the slow arm rotation during the leap with a held coil (fist already back) and a hard cut to the extension.
  The swing happens across a cut in 1-3 f.
- Knockback: on the frame after the impact frames the target is already displaced. Replace the 0.75 s of near-still
  slow motion with about 8 f (0.27 s) of slow but clearly moving contact (head and torso yielding, particles drifting),
  then full speed.
- After the haymaker, consider a carry (attacker travels with the fist planted for 5 fast frames, then about 10 slow
  ones) into a wall or the ground, filmed side-on from a locked camera with the destination in frame.
- Rush: film it side-on or toward camera, never from behind. Use a short burst of afterimage/smear on entry, then a slow
  hang right before contact.
- Flurry: build it as a loop of about 8 short poses from 2-3 camera angles, cut every 2-3 f, the attacker's off-arm
  flung back each time, the target staying put and square.

## F. The six questions

1. **Facing at contact.**
   - Flurry stomach blow (18.52, 19.09, 19.69): Tank-Top's chest is square to the fist and to the camera, and Garou's
     back is to the camera. The blow lands on the FRONT of the stomach (solar plexus, blue spark).
   - Flurry face/chest blow (18.85-18.92, 19.42-19.49): Tank-Top's torso is about 3/4 to camera, chest facing Garou,
     who is side-on on the right. It lands on the front of the chest/neck.
   - Final punch: one shot earlier (21.76-21.82) his face is already turned 3/4 toward the fist with his eyes on it.
     At contact (21.99-22.22) the fist lands on the front-side of the jaw on the side nearest Garou. His face is turned
     INTO the blow, not side-on.
   - In the carry (22.52) his torso is square to Garou, folded over the arm, and side-on to the camera.
2. **Launch distance.** There is no free launch. On screen the final punch moves Tank-Top about 1 body height
   horizontally (from off-frame right to the wall at left-centre), level or slightly down, never up. The first 5 f are
   a fast smear that covers almost all of the distance; the next 10 f creep the last few percent; the wall hits at
   22.756, 24 f (0.8 s) after the impact frame. Garou travels with him (a carry). How far it was from the punch spot to
   the wall is not shown. The wall break ends the move, and there is no next attack. The floor slam (20.0-20.65) drives
   the head down, not away.
3. **Rush readability.** There is no run or dash in this reference. The closest is the carry (22.26-22.72). It reads
   through: a locked side-on camera perpendicular to the travel; the destination (wall) framed before arrival; a 5 f
   horizontal smear of both bodies; a light trail from Garou's eye; horizontal speed streaks behind; then a 10 f slow
   hang so the pose can be read before the wall. The slip under the overhand (6.74-7.44, 22 f) reads from a high angle
   with a continuous body turn.
4. **Dash into the final strike.** There is no travel before the punch. Garou is stationary in the coil (15 f, the
   hands circling, the fist loaded low), then a cut to his eyes (11 f, with the 3 f flare), then 1 f of the fully
   extended thrust and a 2 f fist close-up. The arm is never shown rotating: the transition from loaded to extended is
   carried by cuts and lasts 1-3 frames. The travel happens after contact, as the carry.
5. **Knockback onset.** The fist arrives as a 3 f splash at 21.855, the 1 f impact frame is at 21.955, and on the very
   next frame (21.99) the head is already pushed back. There is no frozen hit-stop. The 8 f after the impact frame are
   slow motion with the body moving slowly (new drawings at 21.99, 22.09 and 22.19; the head drifts back and up; sparks
   and blood drift). The cut at 22.26 (9 f after the impact frame) brings full speed.
6. **Smoothness.**
   - Holds always contain motion: the coil has rotating spirals and moving hands; the eye shot has a flare and flicker;
     the face holds have blood drops and speed lines; the carry's slow hang has streaks and a body creep of a few
     pixels per drawing; the onlooker shot trucks steadily.
   - The camera moves only in eased ways: the barrage reveal is ease-out over about 10 f, the onlooker truck is steady,
     and the coil's pull-out lasts 2 f into a cut. During impacts the camera is locked and the bodies or cuts do the
     moving.
   - Overlap in the bodies: the off-arm flings back on every flurry blow and at the wall break; the head leads and the
     body follows on the face punch.
   - Contrast: very long holds (35-82 f) sit against bursts of 1-3 f shots. The holds themselves sit at 2-3 f per
     drawing; only the impact frames are single.
