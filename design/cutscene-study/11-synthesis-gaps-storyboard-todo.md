# Haymaker Rush kits-8a: reference findings, gap analysis, storyboard, to-do

Written 2026-11-18. Sources:
- One frame-by-frame report per reference, `01-...` to `10-...` in this folder, each with sections A-F.
- Our own map and diagnosis: `00-current-cutscene-and-diagnosis.md`.

All references were viewable: the 10 videos were re-downloaded at 720p (or the upload's best) and read as 12 fps
overview sheets, then every frame inside the events. Limits on what each one shows are in its report's header (for
example, Azure is a 360-px phone capture, so its torso facing can't be judged; Acheron's target never reacts).

## 1. What the references agree on (main findings)

1. **The chest is square to the blow at contact, folded ONTO the fist.**
   - Garou vs Stinger 7.27-8.95 (belly down onto the rising arm), Mumen Rider 2.23 (doubles over the fist), Human
     Battery F3 18.40-18.45 (the target turns square first), Tanktop (folded over the arm at the wall), Jotaro (front faces
     the fist and the camera), Strongest Battlegrounds (chest front every time).
   - Contacts are usually filmed from behind or over the attacker's shoulder, so the chest faces the fist AND the camera
     (Jotaro, Serious Punch 6.52-6.67, Strongest Battlegrounds).
2. **The knockback starts on the next image.**
   - 1-2 frames: Azure 4.27→4.30, Garou vs Stinger 11.88→11.92, Tanktop (frame after the impact frame), Jotaro (3-6
     frames, out of frame in about 15).
   - Where there is a pause, the picture keeps changing (Human Battery F3: 1.6 s of pulsing lines and cards; Mumen: 0.57 s
     freeze with effects animating), or the body keeps MOVING in slow motion (Garou vs Stinger 4.62-5.57).
   - Drawn impact frames are not a frozen body: Azure resumes "with the target already far away".
3. **The strike is loaded BEFORE the travel, held through it, released in 1-3 frames.**
   - Azure 4.83-5.27, Human Battery F3 17.80-18.43, Garou vs Stinger 11.38-11.88, Mumen 3.10-3.52, Tanktop 20.69,
     Jotaro's dive 9.45-9.93, Strongest Battlegrounds 10.30.
   - No reference shows a slow arm rotation on screen. R6 punches are the torso turning behind an arm already aimed
     (Mumen F4).
4. **Launches go up and back, and the next shot shows the gap before the attacker crosses it** (Azure 4.27-5.27).
   Distances: Glasses about 5 body heights a second (Garou vs Stinger), Jotaro more than 5 body heights, Human Battery
   F3 about 6, Strongest Battlegrounds about 3.
5. **Rushes read from setup and camera, not pose detail.**
   - Short dashes are a held pose plus a smear (Azure 3.57-3.70, 4-5 f; Garou vs Stinger 11.32-11.38; Strongest
     Battlegrounds 0.4 s with a ground ring and streak).
   - Longer ones show the run with a camera at the front or alongside, the attacker coming AT the lens: Garou vs Stinger
     0.90-2.73 (head-on), Human Battery F3 17.43-18.10 (striding, fist cocked, orbit to the front), Jotaro 9.45-9.93 (dive
     at the lens).
6. **Nothing is ever fully still, and camera moves are whips into drifts.**
   - Orlando: whips of 0.1-0.25 s easing out into 2-15 % width/s drifts; near-still only for ~0.4 s before the biggest
     hits. Azure: ease-out pull-backs, slow roll and orbit.
   - Every hold has something moving (lines, smoke, flares, camera drift: all ten).

### 3.4 The six problems: confirmed causes

All confirmed by measurement or code. The one hypothesis tested and REJECTED was anchoring.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Torso not facing the fist | CONFIRMED: `AnimController.HitLanded` played the heavy flinch on the target at the uppercut and haymaker (no pose clip held it), turning the chest ~30 deg on the contact frame; the uppercut's +25 deg tilt arched the chest away; the uppercut swing was held at clip 0.066 with the fist still under the belly | the Fold pose clip from each contact; tilts and spacing solved on the baked clips; uppercut held at 0.075 |
| 2 | Launch too short for a long dash | CONFIRMED: the keys threw the target straight up (0.2 studs back) | 9.5 studs back; the dash now covers 7.8 across and 9.3 in all |
| 3 | Rush reads strangely | CONFIRMED: a held stride over 10 studs (skating), the Sprint hidden under an Action-priority clip, a camera straight behind | RushRun over the Sprint; the camera at the front |
| 4 | Arm rotating between dash and punch | CONFIRMED: the coil spread over the rise, a back-to-front aim change (keyed arms blend their direction, so it is a swing), and LeapUp/HaymakerUp overlapping | the load, hold, drive clip, its aim held constant; no overlap |
| 5 | Knockback delayed | CONFIRMED: 0.75 s of `HAY_FREEZE` at 0.5 studs/s, the blast's ease-out stall and the flight's speed jump and turn snap. REJECTED: anchoring (the dummy measured unanchored on server and client; CFrame drives the body anyway) | knockback from the contact frame; `Ultimates.flight` continuity |
| 6 | Stiff camera and bodies | CONFIRMED: linear keys and shots, dead-still holds, a camera riding the body | eased keys and moves, creeps in holds, a panning dash shot |

Also fixed: bystanders saw the dummy target with no tilt (`DummyService` posed it without `targetTilt`).
