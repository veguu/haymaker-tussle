Play Show

At rest this is one busy moment of a fight, so you can see that nothing overlaps. PRESS THE HUD ON THE SCREEN ITSELF: Q and R go on cooldown, the dial revs, Punch throws the big-hit flash, ULT READY uses the ultimate, a fighter's card or the feed plays a knockout. The buttons below play the rest.

Open Play Summon a

The menu buttons on the screen work too. An open window stands clear of the menu buttons; the tools on the right hide under it, and the world behind it goes soft. "Summon a ..." plays the roll for that rarity so you can see and hear each one: Common and Rare are the same, Epic holds its breath, Legendary builds up, Mythic builds longer and goes dark first. "Show the summon warning" puts a Legendary in slot 1 and presses SUMMON: a red WARNING card shows what you will lose (by default for Epic and above; switch it off or choose the rarities in Settings). There is no skip button: pressing anywhere to skip brings up the Robux purchase; "Skip pass owned" shows it once bought (then a press just skips).

How it moves

  • Banners and signs slam in from the left, hold, and slide out to the right. Fast: under a fifth of a second.
  • Countdown: plain white numbers, no frame, appearing out of thin air in the middle of the screen, big but not filling it, one at a time; then the letters of TUSSLE! slam in one after another and settle. No lines, pills or confetti.
  • The Sky brawl print (gloves, clouds, the windmill, stars) drifts slowly inside every piece, sized to the piece; bright stripes slide across the buttons you press.
  • Ability rings drain and refill; a button that comes ready pops once. The speed dial's ring, number and colour follow your speed.
  • Windows slide in from the right and their title slams down; the picked tile lifts.
  • A knocked-out fighter's card gets a red cross drawn over it, sinks, and the row closes up.
  • Big-hit flash: the whole screen in the hitter's colour for about a tenth of a second, under the HUD. Nothing else is ever full-screen.
  • Everything moves (your rule, 2026-11-24): the dots drift on every plate, card, tile and window; a shine crosses only the IMPORTANT pieces (menu buttons, banners, the buttons you act with), rarely (once every 11 to 17 seconds) and each on its own clock; menu buttons and station icons float; crowns bob; ULT READY and SUMMON breathe.
  • Classes screen: the camera pushes in, the slot badges pop in one after another, the rarities slide in from the right, the bottom pieces rise. Press SUMMON: the roll takes the whole screen, classes roll past faster then slower (about four seconds), and it stops on yours in its rarity's colour. A Legendary first climbs RARE, EPIC, LEGENDARY on a hidden card, shaking harder each step, and reveals with a shockwave ring, a glow and twinkles; a Mythic climbs, goes dark, a heartbeat, then breaks with a flash in its colour, a quake, four rings, double rays, twinkles everywhere and confetti. Then the new class lands in your slot with a hit. The (i) tips pop.
  • One spring, one hover, one press for everything: a piece that arrives goes a little past and settles; hovering lifts and brightens it; pressing squashes it. Sound on every one of them (the Sound switch above).
  • Closing (your rule, 2026-11-24): nothing just vanishes. A window, the Classes screen, the quests clipboard, the spectate panel and the warning and skip cards all leave the same way: sink a little, shrink a little, fade, in under a fifth of a second; the camera eases back out of the Classes screen.
  • The last five (your rule, 2026-11-24): at 5 or fewer standing the row at the top gets bigger and pops in once. No red and no beating.
  • Reduced Motion (the switch above): everything appears and leaves without moving, and the flash does not show.

Play them on the Fight and Lobby tabs. In the game each is a tween or one shared loop that only runs while its piece is on screen.
