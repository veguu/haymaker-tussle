Haymaker HUD: a new skin

Built from your three screenshots and your icon sheets. I call it Tinted glass: every piece is one colour in three strengths (a dark see-through fill, a bright edge, a bright icon standing out of it), windows are near-black with a jagged blade for a title, and only the thing you press is a bright striped button.

My reading of "change everything, I like the structure": every piece keeps its place on the screen, and everything about how it looks is new: shapes, lettering, textures, icons. One layout change, because all three of your references do it: the menu is a block of six tiles, not a list of long buttons.

This is a style board, three screens. If you like it, it goes onto the whole HUD mock. Names, Coins and timers are samples.

The fist print: you picked C3, Sky brawl

You picked fists and stars, without the random lines and the lightning bolt. The gloves stay in all three; what goes with them is different in each. The print takes the colour of its piece, drifts slowly, and scales with the piece: small on a small button, big on a window. Press a button under them to see one on all three screens, then tell me which, or name things to add or drop.

Icons in this style

The icons are the set you saw (style A). On a piece they take that piece's colour, so the button and its icon read as one thing. Coins, Gems, the crown and the eight classes keep their own colours, because they are things you own.

What I took from where

  • From the green teleport screenshot: dark see-through pieces in their own colour with a bright edge, a faint pattern inside, the icon bigger than its bar and sticking out, a small label tucked on the bar's corner.
  • From the blue shop screenshot: the near-black window with a jagged blade title that breaks out of it, square menu tiles with the name on the bottom edge, cards that each have their own colour and a big picture on the right, green price buttons.
  • From the fish collection screenshot: the diagonal glass streak across every piece, the round red close button with a white ring, bright bars for tabs.
  • From your icon sheets: the chunky cut icons with a thick outline.
  • For a brawler: the pattern is sparks, bolts and speed lines, the blade title looks like an impact, icons tilt as if mid-swing.
  • New lettering: chunky and rounded, upright. The heavy italic went with the old look; say if you want it back.
  • Kept: the Kidcore colours and round thumb buttons for the fight.
